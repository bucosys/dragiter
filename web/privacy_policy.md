## 3. Data Collection on Our Website

### A. Server Log Files
When you access our Website, information is automatically sent to the server by your browser and temporarily stored in system log files. This data includes:

- IP address of the requesting device
- Date and time of access
- Name and URL of the retrieved file
- Website from which access is made (Referrer URL)
- Browser type, version, and operating system used
- Name of your access provider

**Legal Basis:** The processing is carried out pursuant to **Art. 6 (1) lit. f GDPR** based on our legitimate interest in ensuring system security, technical stability, and server performance monitoring.

**Retention Period:** Server logs are stored temporarily and automatically deleted after a technical retention period (typically 7 to 30 days), unless further retention is required for security incident investigations.

### B. Contact via Email
If you contact us via email, the personal data provided (such as your name, email address, and message content) will be stored and processed solely for the purpose of answering your inquiry and any follow-up questions.

**Legal Basis:** Processing is based on **Art. 6 (1) lit. b GDPR** (if related to pre-contractual measures or software support) or **Art. 6 (1) lit. f GDPR** (legitimate interest in processing user requests).

## 7. Your Rights Under the GDPR

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

1. **Right of Access (Art. 15 GDPR):** Right to obtain confirmation as to whether personal data concerning you is being processed and access to such data.
2. **Right to Rectification (Art. 16 GDPR):** Right to demand the immediate correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
3. **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten") (Art. 17 GDPR):** Right to request the deletion of your personal data under certain conditions.
4. **Right to Restriction of Processing (Art. 18 GDPR):** Right to request the restriction of processing under statutory conditions.
5. **Right to Data Portability (Art. 20 GDPR):** Right to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.
6. **Right to Object (Art. 21 GDPR):** Right to object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to the processing of personal data based on Art. 6 (1) lit. f GDPR.
7. **Right to Lodge a Complaint:** Right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority (e.g., the State Data Protection Commissioner of your German federal state).
